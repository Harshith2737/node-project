PLAN WITH CARE

Before you go

A few essentials for a smoother visit. Always confirm local arrangements with official sources before departure.

◷

Check current details

Opening days, entry rules, access roads and local advisories can change. Confirm details with the site or official tourism portal before travelling.

Official Telangana Tourism ↗
♧

Respect natural places

Use marked trails, keep away from restricted water edges, never feed wildlife and carry reusable water containers.

State tourism overview ↗
⌂

Support local communities

Choose locally owned stays, local guides and direct purchases from craft makers. Ask permission before photographing people or private workspaces.

Tourism circuits and policy ↗
✧

Make heritage last

Do not touch fragile carvings, write on monuments or remove natural and archaeological objects. Follow signage and conservation staff instructions.

Archaeological Survey of India ↗
Seasonal note: waterfalls, reservoirs and forest routes can have changing conditions during and after monsoon periods. Check local weather and access notices; never enter fast-flowing water.
